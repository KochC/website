What is FreeSlots?

FreeSlots reads your existing calendars and turns them into clean, shareable free time. Pick a range, see your open slots, tap Copy, paste into Messages, WhatsApp, or Mail. FreeSlots doesn't schedule meetings, send invitations, or expose your event names to anyone — it only ever works with start times, end times, and whether you're busy.

Highlights

  • Reads your calendars read-only — never creates or edits events
  • Copy or share your availability as clean plain text, ready to paste
  • Custom working hours, buffers, minimum slot length, and time rounding
  • Clean, Compact, or Conversational output styles
  • No account, no backend, no ads, no analytics — works fully offline
