# Support

We're happy to help.

Email [feedback@koch.codes](mailto:feedback@koch.codes) and we'll get back to you. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

## What is FreeSlots?

FreeSlots reads your calendars and shows the gaps between events as clean, shareable availability, so you can answer "when are you free?" in seconds without checking your calendar manually.

## How do I get started?

On first launch, grant calendar access, then choose which calendars count as "busy" and set your working hours. FreeSlots defaults to Mon–Fri 09:00–17:00 with a 30-minute minimum free slot — adjust either in Settings.

## Why don't I see any free time?

Check that calendar access is enabled in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars → FreeSlots, that the right calendars are selected in FreeSlots' own Settings, and that your working hours for that day aren't disabled (e.g. weekends are off by default).

## Does FreeSlots see my event details?

No. FreeSlots only ever looks at start times, end times, and whether an event marks you as busy. It never reads or shares event titles, locations, notes, or attendees. See the [Privacy Policy](https://koch.codes/apps/freeslots/privacy.html) for details.

## Can FreeSlots create or edit calendar events?

No. FreeSlots only requests read access and never writes to your calendar.

## Does it work offline?

Yes. FreeSlots needs no internet connection and no account.
