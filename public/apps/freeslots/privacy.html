Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

FreeSlots does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no backend, and no network connection of any kind — the app works fully in Airplane Mode. Your calendar stays on your iPhone.

Data collection

Data Not Collected. The developer does not collect any data from this app. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking.

Calendar access

FreeSlots requests read-only access to your calendars (EventKit) to determine when you're busy. It calculates free time from event start times, end times, availability status, and all-day/recurrence rules only. Event titles, attendee names, locations, notes, and meeting links are never read into the calculation, never displayed, and never included in anything you copy or share. FreeSlots never requests write access and never creates, edits, or deletes calendar events.

On-device data

Your preferences — selected calendars, working hours, minimum slot length, buffer, time rounding, and output style — are stored locally on your device and never leave it. The free slots shown on screen are calculated fresh each time from your calendars and are not saved anywhere.

Copy & Share

When you tap Copy or Share, FreeSlots places plain text describing your free time on the clipboard or opens Apple's standard share sheet. What you do with that text afterwards (e.g. pasting it into Messages) is entirely up to you and happens outside the app.

Networking

FreeSlots makes no network requests. There are no remote endpoints, background network activity, or third-party services of any kind.

Diagnostics

If you opt in to "Share With App Developers" in iOS Settings, Apple may deliver aggregated, on-device crash and performance diagnostics to the developer through Apple's own systems. This is Apple's processing, not data collected by the app, and it is off by default.

Children

FreeSlots is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
