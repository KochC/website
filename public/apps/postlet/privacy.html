Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Postlet prints personal letters for the address lists you choose. There is no account, no backend and no analytics. Your addresses stay on your device.

Data collection

Data Not Collected. The developer does not collect any data from this app. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs and no tracking. Nothing you type, import or print is sent to the developer.

Contacts

Postlet reads your contacts only when you open the picker and select people yourself. It reads only the postal address of the contacts you pick, uses it to lay out the letter, and never modifies, syncs or uploads your address book. Contact access is requested the first time you open the picker, and the app works fully without it: you can type addresses or import a CSV instead.

Addresses and lists

Addresses you enter, import or pick are stored locally on your device so a list can be reused. You can delete any saved list, or all saved addresses, at any time in Settings. Exported .postlet files are written only where you choose to save them.

Printing

Letters are rendered as PDF on your device and handed to Apple's system print or share sheet. Whatever you do there — print, save to Files, send by Mail — is handled by iOS under your control. Postlet never transmits the document itself.

Camera

Postlet does not use the camera.

Networking

Postlet makes no network requests at all. There is no server and works fully offline, including printing.

Diagnostics

If you opt in to "Share With App Developers" in your device settings, Apple may deliver aggregated crash and performance diagnostics to the developer through Apple's own systems. This is Apple's processing, not data collected by the app, and it is off by default.

Children

Postlet is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
