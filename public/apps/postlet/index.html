What is Postlet?

You have something to say to twelve people, and you want each of them to get a real letter rather than a forwarded email. Postlet writes it once and prints it twelve times: same text, each page addressed to one person, laid out to DIN 5008 Form B so it shows through a standard window envelope.

The preview is drawn from the same geometry the PDF uses, so what you see on screen is what comes out of the printer — including the envelope window that the address has to fit inside.

Highlights

  • DIN 5008 Form B layout: 25 mm margin, address field at 45 mm, 20 mm filing margin
  • One letter per recipient, each starting on its own page
  • {name} in the salutation or body becomes that recipient's first name
  • Window envelopes: DIN lang, C6/5, C6, C5 — the preview shows where the window lands
  • Fold marks and punch hole at their real positions, so folding is not guesswork
  • Contacts picker, CSV import and saved lists for the same people next year
  • Vector PDF, so text stays sharp at any printer resolution
  • Buy once. No subscription, no in-app purchase, no account
  • No ads, no analytics — addresses never leave your device
