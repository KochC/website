What is Postlet?

Pick recipients, choose your sheet, print. Labels are drawn as vector graphics at exact page positions, so they line up instead of drifting. One personalised letter per recipient follows the same list.

Highlights

  • Address labels for any A4 or Letter sheet, at exact positions
  • One personalised letter per recipient, DIN 5008 Form B for window envelopes
  • Fold marks that match the envelope you actually post in
  • Contacts picker, CSV import with column mapping, saved lists
  • Per-printer alignment correction — print a test sheet, save the offset
  • Print on a partly used sheet by choosing a start cell
  • Free: up to 10 recipients per job, unlimited return-address sheets
  • Postlet Pro (€9.99 once): no limit, saved lists, CSV import, more sheets, per-printer alignment
  • No account, no ads, no analytics — addresses never leave your device
