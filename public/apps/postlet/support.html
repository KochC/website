Support

We're happy to help.

Email feedback@koch.codes and we'll get back to you. Please include your printer model and the label sheet you used if you're reporting an alignment problem. Please don't send real address lists.

How do I print labels?

Open Postlet, choose Address labels, add recipients from Contacts, type them in or import a CSV, then pick your sheet and tap Preview. From there you can print or share the PDF.

My labels are slightly off the sheet

That is normal — every printer shifts slightly. Open Test sheet & alignment, print the test sheet on plain paper, hold it against a real label sheet and compare the cell edges. Then save a correction for that printer. Each printer can have its own.

Printing at 100%

In the print dialog choose 100% or Actual Size. "Fit to Page" rescales the page and moves every label, so nothing lines up.

What is a partial sheet?

If you have already used some labels, choose a start cell. The labels before that cell stay empty, exactly like on paper, so you don't waste a sheet.

Which envelope should I choose?

Pick the one you actually post in. Postlet lays the letter out to DIN 5008 Form B (a German standard) and prints fold marks for that envelope, then shows the window in the preview so you can confirm the address will be visible.

How does {name} work?

Write the letter once and use {name} wherever the first name belongs — in the salutation or the body. Each recipient then gets their own letter. If a name is a company or a single word, Postlet uses the whole name instead of guessing a first name.

What is free, what is Pro?

Free prints up to 10 recipient labels or letters per job, and return-address sheets are always unlimited. Postlet Pro is a one-time purchase (no subscription) that removes the limit and adds saved lists, CSV import, more label sheets, your own sheet measurements and per-printer alignment. Restore purchase is on the Pro screen.

Do my addresses leave my device?

No. There is no account and no server, and no address is ever uploaded. You can delete every saved address in Settings.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Postlet needs no internet connection. iOS contacts the App Store only to load or restore the purchase.
