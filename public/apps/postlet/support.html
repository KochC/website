Support

We're happy to help.

Email feedback@koch.codes and we'll get back to you. Please include your printer model and the envelope you used if you're reporting a layout problem. Please don't send real address lists.

How do I print letters?

Open Postlet, tap Write a letter, add recipients from Contacts, type them in or import a CSV, then tap Write the letter. Fill in the salutation, subject and body, then tap Preview. From there you can print or share the PDF.

What does {name} do?

Write the letter once and use {name} wherever the first name belongs — in the salutation or the body. Each recipient then gets their own letter. If a name is a company or a single word, Postlet uses the whole name instead of guessing a first name.

Which envelope should I choose?

Pick the one you actually post in. Postlet lays the letter out to DIN 5008 Form B (a German standard) and prints fold marks for that envelope, then shows the window in the preview so you can confirm the address will be visible. If the preview warns that an address does not fit the window, shorten it or choose a larger envelope.

Where do the fold marks go?

The preview draws the same marks the PDF prints, in the left filing margin. Fold there and the address block ends up in the window of the envelope you selected.

Printing at 100%

In the print dialog choose 100% or Actual Size. "Fit to Page" rescales the page and moves the address block, so it may no longer line up with the envelope window.

My letter is slightly off

Check that the print dialog is not set to scale the page, and that your printer is feeding A4 rather than US Letter. Postlet draws everything at the exact physical page size, so a correct printer needs no adjustment.

What is writing help?

The wand button in the letter editor can draft a letter from a short description, suggest subject lines, and rewrite the body in a different tone. It runs on your device using Apple Intelligence, so nothing is uploaded. Every suggestion is shown first and only added when you tap Use this text.

How does proofreading work?

Check spelling and grammar reads the letter on your device and marks the passages it thinks are wrong, with a one-sentence explanation for each. It never rewrites the letter: a correction is only applied when you tap it, and anything it cannot suggest a fix for is left to you.

Why is writing help missing?

It needs Apple Intelligence: iOS 26 or later and an iPhone that supports it. On other devices the wand offers only Fill in names and signature, which works everywhere. Everything else in the app is unaffected.

Do I have to pay again?

No. Postlet is a one-time purchase. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no account — every feature is available the first time you open the app.

Do my addresses leave my device?

No. There is no account and no server, and no address is ever uploaded. You can delete every saved address in Settings.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Postlet makes no network requests, so writing a letter, laying it out, printing it and sharing the PDF all work in airplane mode. Writing help and proofreading also run offline once Apple Intelligence is set up — iOS downloads that model itself when you turn the feature on.
