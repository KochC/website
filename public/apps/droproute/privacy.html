Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

DropRoute is designed to operate entirely on your device. There is no account, no backend, and no network connection required to use the app.

Data collection

Data Not Collected. DropRoute does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking.

Your files

Files you share to DropRoute are copied directly into the folders you chose — on your device or in a storage provider you already use through the Files app (for example iCloud Drive or a network share). DropRoute never sends your files to us or to anyone else, and does not keep copies of them. Temporary copies used during saving are deleted immediately afterwards.

Folder access

To save into a folder, DropRoute stores a system-provided access reference ("bookmark") for each folder you add. These references and your route settings (name, icon, naming rule) are stored only on your device, in storage shared between the app and its share extension. They never leave the device. Deleting a route or the app removes them.

Purchases

DropRoute Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. We receive no personal or payment information. The app checks your purchase using Apple's on-device StoreKit records.

Accounts

DropRoute does not require an account.

Networking

DropRoute makes no network requests of its own. Network traffic only occurs if you save to a cloud or network location, and then it is handled by that provider through the Files app, not by DropRoute.

Diagnostics

If you opt in to "Share With App Developers" in iOS Settings, Apple may deliver aggregated crash and performance diagnostics to the developer through Apple's own systems. This is Apple's processing, not data collected by the app, and it is off by default.

Children

DropRoute is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If DropRoute ever adds analytics, crash reporting, or any other data-handling capability, this policy — and the corresponding App Store privacy answers — will be updated first, with a new "Last updated" date.
