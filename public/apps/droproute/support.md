# Support

We're happy to help.

Email [feedback@koch.codes](mailto:feedback@koch.codes) and we'll get back to you. Please include your device model, iOS version, and the kind of folder you save to (On My iPhone, iCloud Drive, network share, external drive) if you're reporting a problem.

## How do I use DropRoute?

Open DropRoute and add a route: give it a name and choose a folder in Files. Then, in any app, tap **Share**, choose **DropRoute**, and tap the route. The file is copied into that folder.

## I don't see DropRoute in the share sheet.

Scroll the row of apps to the end and tap **More**. Turn on DropRoute and drag it toward the top so it shows up first next time.

## A route shows "Repair". What happened?

DropRoute can't reach that folder right now — it may have been moved, renamed or deleted, or the network share or external drive is disconnected. Tap the route, tap the folder, and choose it again. Network shares and external drives may need repairing after they reconnect.

## Does DropRoute move or change my original file?

No. DropRoute saves a copy into the chosen folder; the original stays where it was.

## What happens if a file with the same name exists?

By default DropRoute keeps both and names the new one "Name 2". With Pro you can choose per route to replace the existing file instead.

## What does DropRoute Pro include?

A one-time purchase, no subscription: unlimited routes (free includes two), file name templates such as `{date} {name}`, renaming right in the share sheet, and the Replace option. If you reinstall or use a new device, tap **Restore Purchase** on the Pro screen.

## Can I use DropRoute with Shortcuts?

Yes. Use the **Save Files to Route** action in the Shortcuts app.

## Does DropRoute require an internet connection?

No. DropRoute works fully offline and has no account, ads, analytics, or cloud service. See the [Privacy Policy](https://koch.codes/apps/droproute/privacy.html).
