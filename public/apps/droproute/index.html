What is DropRoute?

Saving a file to the right folder on iPhone and iPad usually means Share → Save to Files → Location → Folder → Subfolder → Save.

DropRoute turns your most-used Files folders into big one-tap buttons in the share sheet: Share → DropRoute → Invoices. Done.

Highlights

  • Your favorite folders as one-tap destinations in any app's share sheet
  • Works with On My iPhone/iPad, iCloud Drive and other Files locations
  • Never overwrites by accident — duplicates are kept as "Invoice 2.pdf"
  • One-tap repair if a folder was moved or a drive disconnected
  • Shortcuts action "Save Files to Route"
  • Pro (one-time purchase): unlimited routes, file name templates, rename while saving
  • No account, no backend, no ads, no analytics — works fully offline
