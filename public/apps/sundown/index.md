# Sundown

A quieter end to the day.

A bedside sunset light: warm light, then deep red, then dark. Collects no data, works fully offline.

- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sundown-sleep-light/id6807182599
- iPhone
- iOS 18 or later
- Lifestyle

## What is Sundown?

Your iPhone becomes a bedside sunset — warm light, then deep red, then dark. Stand it on a bedside surface, tap Start, and let the room dim gently as you fall asleep.

## Highlights

- Gentle warm-to-red-to-dark sunset using your screen and rear light
- Preset lengths plus a one-tap "+10 min" extension
- Optional bedtime reminder notification — it only reminds you, it never starts a sunset on its own
- **Sundown Pro** adds Shortcuts/Siri Start, custom sunset profiles (Gentle, Deep Red, Long Evening), and custom extension lengths from 3–30 minutes
- No account, no ads, no analytics — works fully offline

## Screenshots

- Controls: time left, +10 minutes, Brighter, End
- Room Light settings: dim a HomeKit light along with the wall glow
