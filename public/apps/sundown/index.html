What is Sundown?

Your iPhone becomes a bedside sunset — warm light, then deep red, then dark. Stand it on a bedside surface, tap Start, and let the room dim gently as you fall asleep.

Highlights

  • Gentle warm-to-red-to-dark sunset using your screen and rear light
  • Preset lengths plus a one-tap "+10 min" extension
  • Optional bedtime reminder notification — it only reminds you, it never starts a sunset on its own
  • Sundown Pro (one-time unlock) adds Shortcuts/Siri Start, custom sunset profiles (Gentle, Deep Red, Long Evening), and custom extension lengths from 3–30 minutes
  • No account, no ads, no analytics — works fully offline
