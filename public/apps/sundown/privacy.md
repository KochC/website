# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Sundown does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All settings remain on your device. The app requires no account and makes no network connections. Camera access is used only to operate the rear light during a sunset; no images are captured or processed. Optional local notifications are scheduled on your device only if you enable a bedtime reminder.

## Data collection

**Data Not Collected.** The developer does not collect any data from this app. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking.

## On-device data

Your preferences (such as sunset length and glow calibration) are stored locally using the app's own settings storage and never leave your device.

## Camera

Camera permission is used solely to control the rear torch for indirect wall lighting during a sunset. No camera imagery is captured, stored, transmitted, or analyzed.

## Notifications

If you turn on the optional bedtime reminder, a local notification is scheduled on your device. It never starts a sunset on its own. Declining the permission does not affect the rest of the app.

## Networking

Sundown makes no network requests and works fully in Airplane Mode. There are no remote endpoints or background network activity.

## Diagnostics

Crash and performance diagnostics use Apple's on-device MetricKit and system logging. Nothing is stored or transmitted by the app. If you opt in to "Share With App Developers" in iOS, Apple may deliver aggregated crash reports to the developer through Apple's own systems; this is Apple's processing, not data collected by the app.

## Children

Sundown is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
