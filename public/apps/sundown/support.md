# Support

We're happy to help.

Email [feedback@koch.codes](mailto:feedback@koch.codes) and we'll get back to you. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

## How do I start a sunset?

Open the app, choose a length, and tap **Start Sundown**. Stand the phone on a bedside surface with the screen facing the bed and the rear light aimed at a nearby wall. Keep it ventilated and clear of blankets.

## Does it start on its own?

No. The optional bedtime reminder only reminds you; it never starts a sunset automatically.

## Does it work offline?

Yes. Sundown needs no account and no internet, and works fully in Airplane Mode.

## Privacy

Sundown collects no data. See the [Privacy Policy](https://koch.codes/apps/sundown/privacy.html).
