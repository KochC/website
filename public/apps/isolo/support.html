Support

We're happy to help.

Email feedback@koch.codes and we'll get back to you. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

How do I cut out an object?

Open Isolo, choose a photo (or take one), then tap the object you want to keep. To refine the edges, drag a box around it or draw around it with the lasso. When the selection looks right, tap Export PNG.

Why can I only select the main subject?

Tapping any object uses Apple's on-device selection model, which requires iOS 27. On earlier iOS versions Isolo can still lift the photo's main subject and export a real transparent PNG. The model may also need a one-time download; Settings shows whether it is installed.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the selection model is installed. Isolo needs no account, uploads nothing and works fully in Airplane Mode.

What does Pro include?

Isolo Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks full-resolution export, combining several objects into one PNG, box and lasso refinements with multiple hints, product-photo backgrounds, and saved selections. The free version stays useful: unlimited photos, one object per photo, no watermark, no ads.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings inside Isolo and tap Restore Purchase. Pro is tied to your Apple Account, so it restores on any iPhone signed in with the same account.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. All processing happens on your iPhone, and Isolo reads only the photos you pick. See the Privacy Policy.

Which photos may I use?

Only photos you own or have permission to share.
