Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Isolo Cutout processes photos entirely on your iPhone. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app requires no account, has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

Data collection

Data Not Collected. The developer does not collect any data from this app. Nothing is tracked and nothing is profiled.

Your photos

Isolo reads only the photos you explicitly pick, or the picture you take with the camera. It keeps a private working copy while you edit, and deletes that copy when you close the photo. Exported PNGs are written only where you choose: Photos, Files, the clipboard or the share sheet. No photo is ever uploaded.

Apple's selection model

Tapping any object uses Apple's on-device selection model, which iOS manages and may download once. That download is between iOS and Apple; Isolo never sends your photos or selections with it. After it completes, everything works offline.

Camera

Camera access is used only when you choose Take Photo, so you can cut an object out of the picture you are about to take. No camera imagery is captured in the background.

Purchases

Isolo Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details. The app only checks whether a valid purchase exists for your Apple Account.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date.
