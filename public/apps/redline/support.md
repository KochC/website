# Support

We're happy to help.

Email [feedback@koch.codes](mailto:feedback@koch.codes) and we'll get back to you. Please include your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac) and system version if you're reporting a problem. Please don't send confidential documents.

## How do I compare two PDFs?

Open Redline, tap *Choose Old Version* and *Choose New Version*. The comparison starts automatically. You can also drop two PDFs onto the window, or share a PDF to Redline from Files or Mail.

## What do the colours mean?

Red marks text that was removed, blue marks text that was added. Each detected change also has a symbol and a label, so you never have to rely on colour. Moved passages and formatting-only changes (bold, italic, size) are labelled separately.

## Why does Redline say "Possible change"?

For scanned pages Redline recognises text on your device. When it isn't sure, it says so instead of guessing.

## What is free, what is Pro?

Free compares documents up to 10 pages, as often as you like. Redline Pro is a one-time purchase that adds unlimited pages, text recognition for scanned PDFs, ignored areas (skip stamps, dates or barcodes on every page), change explanations by Apple Intelligence on your device, and reports without the footer. *Restore Purchases* is in Settings.

## A dated stamp shows up as a change on every page

With Redline Pro, touch and hold the change (right-click on Mac) and choose *Ignore This Area on Every Page*. Undo it in Settings → Ignore.

## Can I rely on Redline for legal review?

Redline is a document comparison tool. It shows the differences it detects; it is not legal advice and does not approve, verify or certify documents. Optional AI explanations can be wrong. For important legal, financial, regulatory or other consequential decisions, review the original documents before relying on the result.

## Does it work offline?

Yes. Redline needs no internet connection and no account. Your documents never leave your device.
