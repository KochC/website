What is Redline Compare?

Choose the old version of a document and the new one. Redline highlights the differences it detects — changed words, added or removed pages, moved passages and visual edits — side by side on iPad and Mac, before/after on iPhone — and lets you export a report.

Highlights

  • Inserted or removed pages don't throw off the rest of the document
  • Reflowed paragraphs don't flood you with false changes
  • Moved passages and formatting changes are labelled as such
  • PDF report and CSV change list export
  • No account, no upload, no subscription — works fully offline
© Christopher Koch · Redline Compare is a local, offline app with no account, no backend, and no tracking.
