# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Redline Compare does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no backend, and no network connection of any kind — the app works fully in Airplane Mode. Your PDFs stay on your device.

## Data collection

**Data Not Collected.** The developer does not collect any data from this app. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking.

## Your documents

Redline opens only the PDFs you choose, drop, or share to it, and reads them on your device to find what changed. It never uploads them, never modifies, moves, or renames your original files, and keeps no copies. Temporary working data is deleted when the comparison ends. Extracted text, file names and comparison results are never logged or sent anywhere.

## Passwords

Passwords for protected PDFs are held in memory only while the document is open. They are never stored, synced, or logged.

## Text recognition and explanations

Text recognition for scanned pages and change explanations (both Redline Pro) use Apple's on-device frameworks (Vision and Apple Intelligence). Nothing is sent to the developer or to third parties.

## Reports

Reports (PDF) and change lists (CSV) are created only when you export them, and saved only where you choose.

## On-device settings

Your preferences — comparison mode, ignore options and ignored areas — are stored locally on your device. They contain no document content.

## Purchases

Redline Pro is a one-time In-App Purchase handled entirely by Apple. The developer receives no payment details and Redline has no account.

## Networking

Redline makes no network requests of its own. There are no remote endpoints, background network activity, or third-party services.

## Diagnostics

If you opt in to "Share With App Developers" in your device settings, Apple may deliver aggregated crash and performance diagnostics to the developer through Apple's own systems. This is Apple's processing, not data collected by the app, and it is off by default.

## Children

Redline is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
