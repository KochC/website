How do I compare two PDFs?

Open Redline, tap Choose Old Version and Choose New Version. The comparison starts automatically. You can also drop two PDFs onto the window, or share a PDF to Redline from Files or Mail.

What do the colours mean?

Red marks text that was removed, blue marks text that was added. Every change also has a symbol and a label, so you never have to rely on colour. Moved passages and formatting-only changes (bold, italic, size) are labelled separately.

Why does Redline say "Possible change"?

For scanned pages Redline recognises text on your device. When it isn't sure, it says so instead of guessing.

What is free, what is Pro?

Free compares documents up to 10 pages, as often as you like. Redline Pro is a one-time purchase that adds unlimited pages, text recognition for scanned PDFs, ignored areas (skip stamps, dates or barcodes on every page) and reports without the footer. Restore Purchases is in Settings.

A dated stamp shows up as a change on every page

With Redline Pro, touch and hold the change (right-click on Mac) and choose Ignore This Area on Every Page. Undo it in Settings → Ignore.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Redline needs no internet connection and no account. Your documents never leave your device.
