What is AngleFold?

AngleFold reads the physical hinge angle of iPhone Duo and turns it into a fast digital angle meter — for checking whether something is approximately 90°, setting a fold or bracket to a target angle, measuring furniture or cabinet geometry, or calculating a basic miter angle.

Why does the app show "Demo Mode" or "iPhone Duo required"?

Live hinge-angle measurement needs the physical hinge sensor built into iPhone Duo. On any other device, AngleFold shows an explicitly labeled Demo Mode with simulated readings, so you can try the interface without a fake live measurement.

How accurate is the measurement?

AngleFold is designed as a convenient everyday measuring tool, not certified metrology equipment. It should not be used where safety or regulatory requirements depend on a calibrated measurement instrument.

What does "Zero" do?

Zero stores the current angle as a reference point and shows all further readings relative to it, until you reset it.

What does "Hold" do?

Hold freezes the currently displayed reading — including any target or miter values derived from it — so you can read it without keeping the device perfectly still.

What is the Miter value?

A basic equal-miter calculation: half of the currently displayed angle. It does not solve compound miters.

Does AngleFold require an internet connection?

No. AngleFold works fully offline and has no account, ads, analytics, or cloud dependency. See the Privacy Policy for details.
