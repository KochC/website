Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

AngleFold is designed to operate entirely on your device. There is no account, no backend, and no network connection of any kind required to use the app.

Data collection

Data Not Collected. AngleFold does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking.

Measurements

Hinge-angle measurements are processed locally on the device and are never transmitted to us or anyone else.

On-device preferences

AngleFold stores a small set of settings — such as your target angle, tolerance, and haptic preference — locally on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults). This data never leaves the device.

Accounts

AngleFold does not require an account.

Networking

AngleFold makes no network requests of its own. There are no remote endpoints, background network activity, or third-party services of any kind.

Diagnostics

If you opt in to "Share With App Developers" in iOS Settings, Apple may deliver aggregated, on-device crash and performance diagnostics to the developer through Apple's own systems. This is Apple's processing, not data collected by the app, and it is off by default.

Children

AngleFold is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If AngleFold ever adds analytics, a crash-reporting service, or any other data-handling capability, this policy — and the corresponding App Store privacy answers — will be updated first, and the update will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: feedback@koch.codes
