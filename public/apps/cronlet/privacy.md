# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Cronlet does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no backend, and the app makes no network connections of its own. Everything stays on your iPhone.

## Data collection

**Data Not Collected.** The developer does not collect any data from this app. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking.

## On-device data

Your jobs — shortcut names, schedules, optional input text — and the run history are stored locally on your device in the app's own storage and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Notifications

Cronlet schedules local notifications on your device to signal that a job is due. They are created and delivered entirely on-device; no push server is involved. Notification permission is requested only when you save your first job.

## Shortcuts

Cronlet does not read your Shortcuts library. The shortcuts you schedule are run by Apple's Shortcuts app through an automation you create yourself.

## Purchases

The optional Cronlet Pro purchase is processed entirely by Apple. The developer receives no personal or payment information.

## Diagnostics

If you opt in to "Share With App Developers" in iOS Settings, Apple may deliver aggregated, on-device crash and performance diagnostics to the developer through Apple's own systems. This is Apple's processing, not data collected by the app, and it is off by default.

## Children

Cronlet is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
